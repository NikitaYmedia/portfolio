KLIP & RO

Bilingual Salon Experience

What the site is for: a modern hair salon website. It presents the services and prices, the team, a photo gallery and contact details, and lets clients book an appointment online. Decisions you made: You designed a simple pink mockup yourself and asked me to turn it into a full project. All visuals, icons and animations are redrawn in CSS only, with no Tailwind and no inline styles. No JavaScript is written inside the HTML. It lives in separate files and only toggles classes and text. The site is multi-page: every item in the menu (Home, Services, About, Gallery, Contact) became a real page filled with text. Images are linked by URL, and the site language is English. You wanted something unusual with a clear structure: a neon sign, a marquee, a style quiz, a booking wizard and a lightbox gallery.
